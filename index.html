Using a song from YouTube or Spotify
  1. YouTube on a phone: phones can’t capture another app’s audio. Play the song out loud from another device, or through your phone speaker from YouTube, and use Record from mic.
  2. YouTube on a computer (Chrome or Edge): open the song in another tab and pause it. Press Capture a browser tab, choose that tab, and make sure Also share tab audio is on. Play the song, then come back and press Stop capture. Up to 6 minutes.
  3. Spotify: its player blocks capture, so a captured Spotify tab comes through silent. Play the song out loud and use Record from mic instead. The same goes for any app outside the browser.
